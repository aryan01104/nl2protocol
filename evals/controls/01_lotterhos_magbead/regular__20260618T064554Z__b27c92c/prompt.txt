Can you create a Python protocol for the Opentrons OT-2 based on this instruction and config?

Instruction:
CLEANUP OF FRAGMENTED DNA (single-sample run on sample_plate)

Setup: sample_plate A1 contains 100 µL of fragmented DNA sample; the sample_plate sits on the magnetic module. reagent_rack A1 contains KAPA Pure Beads (already equilibrated to room temperature and fully resuspended). reagent_rack A2 contains elution buffer (10 mM Tris-HCl, pH 8.0–8.5). ethanol_reservoir A1 contains freshly-prepared 80% ethanol. The purified eluate will be transferred into elution_plate A1.

Protocol:

1. Add 80 µL of KAPA Pure Beads from reagent_rack A1 to the 100 µL fragmented DNA sample in sample_plate A1.

2. Mix thoroughly by pipetting up and down 10 times at 100 µL.

3. Incubate at room temperature for 5 – 15 min to bind the DNA to the beads.

4. Engage the magnetic module; wait until the liquid is clear.

5. Carefully remove and discard the supernatant from sample_plate A1.

6. Keeping the plate on the magnet, add 200 µL of 80% ethanol from ethanol_reservoir A1 to sample_plate A1.

7. Incubate on the magnet at room temperature for ≥30 sec.

8. Carefully remove and discard the ethanol from sample_plate A1.

9. Keeping the plate on the magnet, add a second 200 µL of 80% ethanol from ethanol_reservoir A1 to sample_plate A1.

10. Incubate on the magnet at room temperature for ≥30 sec.

11. Carefully remove and discard the ethanol from sample_plate A1. Try to remove all residual ethanol without disturbing the beads.

12. Dry the beads at room temperature for 3 – 5 min, or until all of the ethanol has evaporated. Caution: over-drying the beads may result in reduced yield.

13. Disengage the magnetic module.

14. Resuspend the beads in an appropriate volume of elution buffer from reagent_rack A2.

15. Incubate at room temperature for 2 min to elute the DNA off the beads. The elution time may be extended up to 10 min if necessary to improve DNA recovery.

16. Engage the magnetic module; wait until the liquid is clear.

17. Transfer the clear supernatant from sample_plate A1 to elution_plate A1.

Tip strategy: use a fresh tip for each clean-reagent addition (beads, ethanol, elution buffer). Reuse a single tip across the supernatant- and ethanol-removal steps for the same well.
