BCA Protein Assay — Microplate Procedure

1. Prepare BSA standard curve by serial dilution of the 2.0 mg/mL BSA stock in sample buffer. Standards: 2000, 1500, 1000, 750, 500, 250, 125, 25, 0 µg/mL.

2. Prepare Working Reagent (WR) by mixing Reagent A and Reagent B at a 50:1 ratio. For a full plate, combine 50 mL Reagent A with 1 mL Reagent B in a reservoir.

3. Pipette 25 µL of each standard and 25 µL of each unknown sample replicate into a microplate well (working range = 20–2000 µg/mL).

4. Add 200 µL of the WR to each well.

5. Mix plate thoroughly on a plate shaker for 30 seconds.

6. Cover the plate and incubate at 37°C for 30 minutes.

7. After incubation, the plate is read at 562 nm on a plate reader (this step is performed off the OT-2).
