qPRC Standrads Preparation

Step 1 — Making combined standrads

To make combined standrads (Dremo+MSX) for multiplex qPRC assay, use recipe below. This makes 0.1 ng/µl combined standard, which will be the top point.

- 1O µl of 1 ng/µl Dremo gBlock standard
- 10 µl of 1 ng/µl MSX gBlock standard
- 80 µl of 1X TE Buffr (pH 8.0)

Step 2 — Serial diluton

To make remaining combined standrads, do a serial diluton of 0.1 ng/µl combined standard (10 µl standard + 9O µl 1X TE Buffer). qPRC standards range from 0.1 ng/µl to 0.0001 pg/µl.
