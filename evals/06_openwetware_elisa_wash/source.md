For this case, **only the wash portion** is the instruction — the surrounding ELISA workflow (coating, blocking, antibodies, detection) is intentionally not included. ELISA wash is a stand-alone repeatable sub-protocol commonly run on OT-2 between manual or assay-machine steps.

---

## Wash section (verbatim)

> Wash the plate 5 times with PBS containing 0.05% Tween 20 (300 µl per well).

Practical guidance from source:

> By using excess volumes (300 µl instead of 200 µl or 100 µl in the other steps), the researcher ensures that no residual molecules cling to the walls of the wells.

> After the washing steps, remove all wash buffer by tapping the plate against absorbent paper. Immediately proceed to the next step of the assay (do not leave the plate to dry).

The Mabtech page does not specify an incubation time per individual wash cycle — only that five total wash cycles occur with 300 µl per well of PBS-T.
