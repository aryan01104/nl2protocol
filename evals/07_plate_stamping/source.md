## Avidien definition (verbatim quotes)

> "The process of transferring one plate's contents to another while maintaining the same well configuration."

> Basic procedure:
> 1. Pull samples from plate one
> 2. Dispense into plate two

> "Stamping sometimes requires transferring the same reagent into all 96 wells from a reservoir, while other times it involves transferring contents between plates where wells contain unique samples that must not be mixed or cross-contaminated."

> Notable challenge: "When stamping a small volume into another liquid, it can often be difficult to visually discern which wells have already been stamped."

The article does not give specific volumes or detailed step-by-step protocols — plate stamping at this level is genuinely simple in description.
